Nib Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Nib collects no data. It has no account, no server and no analytics, and it doesn't send anything you read, select or save anywhere.

What stays on your Mac

  • Translation uses Apple's on-device Translation framework.
  • Explanations, on Macs with Apple Intelligence, use Apple's on-device model.
  • Definitions come from the dictionary built into macOS.
  • Your library (saved words, translations, notes and decks) is stored in a file on your Mac. Nib has no copy of it.

How Nib sees the text you select

Nib never reads other apps' text in the background. It gets the text you select in one of two ways, both only when you ask:

  • Its icon. When you double-click or select text, Nib's icon appears. This uses macOS Input Monitoring to notice mouse clicks only; Nib never sees what you type. When you click the icon, Nib uses Screen Recording to take a picture of that one spot on screen and read the selected words with Apple's on-device text recognition. The picture is used for that lookup only, stays in memory, and is never saved or sent. Nothing is captured until you click the icon.
  • The keyboard shortcut (Shift-Command-K, or Services → Look Up in Nib). macOS hands Nib the selected text directly; no permission is needed.

On iPhone

Nib for iPhone works the same way: translation, explanations and your library stay on your iPhone, and nothing is sent anywhere. It gets English text only in ways you start:

  • What you copied. When you open Nib, it reads the text you copied, only if it was copied since you last left Nib, and translates it on your iPhone. iOS asks your permission before Nib reads it, and you can turn this off in Nib's Settings (Look up what I copy). Text that looks like a card number, IBAN or key is left alone. Copied text is never stored unless you save it.
  • The Share menu (Share → Look Up in Nib) hands Nib the text you selected, for that lookup only.
  • Typing or pasting a word into the Look Up tab.
  • A screenshot, if you set it up. The optional Look Up On Screen shortcut action opens a screenshot in Nib so you can tap its words. Apple's on-device text recognition reads it; the screenshot stays in memory, isn't saved to Photos or your library, and is gone when you close it.

Your library is stored in Nib's own storage on your iPhone. Word reminders are scheduled on the phone itself; there is no push server.

Notifications

If you turn on word reminders, Nib shows your own saved words as notifications. They are created on your device; nothing is sent to Nib's developer or anyone else.

Diagnostics

Nib has an optional, off-by-default diagnostics log for troubleshooting. It records which app a lookup happened in and how many characters were selected, never the text itself, and it stays on your Mac.

Apple services

Translation languages and Apple Intelligence models are downloaded and updated by macOS, under Apple's privacy policy. Nib takes no part in those downloads beyond asking macOS to start one.
