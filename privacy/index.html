Nib Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Nib collects no data. It has no account, no server and no analytics, and it doesn't send anything you read, select or save anywhere.

What stays on your Mac

  • Translation uses Apple's on-device Translation framework.
  • Explanations, on Macs with Apple Intelligence, use Apple's on-device model.
  • Definitions come from the dictionary built into macOS.
  • Your library (saved words, translations, notes and decks) is stored in a file on your Mac. Nib has no copy of it.

Accessibility permission

Nib asks for Accessibility permission for one purpose: when you click its button after selecting text, it sends the Copy command to the app you're reading in, reads the copied text, and puts your clipboard back exactly as it was. Nib copies nothing until you click its button, and macOS never allows copying from password fields.

Notifications

If you turn on word reminders, Nib shows your own saved words as notifications. They are created on your Mac; nothing is sent to Nib's developer or anyone else.

Diagnostics

Nib has an optional, off-by-default diagnostics log for troubleshooting. It records which app a lookup happened in and how many characters were selected, never the text itself, and it stays on your Mac.

Apple services

Translation languages and Apple Intelligence models are downloaded and updated by macOS, under Apple's privacy policy. Nib takes no part in those downloads beyond asking macOS to start one.
