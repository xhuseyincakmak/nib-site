Nib Support

Select a word on your Mac. See it in your language. Keep it.

Questions, bugs or ideas: xhuseyincakmak@gmail.com

Nib's icon doesn't appear, or can't read the text

  • Check that capture is on: click the nib in the menu bar; Capture selections should be switched on.
  • Check both permissions in System Settings → Privacy & Security: Input Monitoring (to show the icon) and Screen & System Audio Recording (to read the text), with Nib switched on in each. After switching them on, quit and reopen Nib.
  • Prefer no permissions? Select text and press Shift-Command-K, or right-click it → Services → Look Up in Nib. (Some apps, like Claude or WhatsApp, don't support Services; the icon works there.)

No translation appears

Nib uses Apple's on-device translation, which needs the language pack for your language. Open Set Up… from Nib's menu and download it, or add your language in System Settings → General → Language & Region → Translation Languages. You can change the language in Nib's Settings.

No explanations

Plain-English explanations use Apple Intelligence, so they need a Mac that supports it, with Apple Intelligence switched on in System Settings. Without it, Nib still translates and shows definitions.

Where is Nib?

Nib lives in the menu bar (a pen-nib icon) and has no Dock icon. Its library opens from the menu bar icon → Open Library.

Privacy

Nib collects no data. Everything runs on your Mac. Read the privacy policy.
